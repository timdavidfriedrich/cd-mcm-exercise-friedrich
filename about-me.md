## Why CD matters for agile teams
Agile development thrives on quick iterations, constant feedback, and adaptability. Continuous Delivery is the technical foundation that makes this possible. It reduces Risk by automating releases, which minimizes human error and makes deployments routine rather than stressful events. Also, delivering smaller increments frequently allows teams to get immediate validation from automated tests. And by automating the deployment pipeline, self-organizing teams can focus on writing quality code instead of dealing with manual operational tasks.
